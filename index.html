Questions

Good questions.

What's a focus cycle?

A set block of work followed by a short break. 25/5 is the classic. 50/10 is for deep work.

What are the breaks?

A 5–10 minute guided meditation, 10 push-ups, a dance on the beat, or a quick stretch.

Do I have to move?

No. Pick the meditation or the stretch if push-ups aren't your thing.

Is it on Android?

iPhone first. Join the waitlist and pick Android. That tells us how many people want it.
